**Critical rule (D-01/Pitfall 2, Phase 4 lesson, applies to every method added below):** every
`ApplyXDowned`/`IsXDowned` delegate passed into `BossRegistry.Register`'s `BossDefinition` MUST be a
named, separately-`[JITWhenModsEnabled("CalamityMod")]`-tagged method -- NEVER an inline lambda. An
inline lambda compiles into a compiler-generated `<>c` cache-class method that does NOT inherit the
enclosing method's `[JITWhenModsEnabled]` attribute, producing a real `JITException` when CalamityMod is
disabled (confirmed live, commit `0e19600`).
