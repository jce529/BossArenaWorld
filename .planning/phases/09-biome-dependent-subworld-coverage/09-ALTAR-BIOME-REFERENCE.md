# Phase 9: Biome Altar & Arena — Reference Research (Pre-Discussion Input)

**Compiled:** 2026-08-13
**Status:** Reference material for `/gsd:discuss-phase 9` — this is curated conversation research, NOT a formal GSD artifact (no CONTEXT.md/RESEARCH.md schema). Treat it as raw input discuss-phase should read and re-verify, not as locked decisions.
**Sources:** Multi-pass wiki research (calamitymod.wiki.gg, infernummod.wiki.gg, spiritmod.wiki.gg/fandom, modofredemption.wiki.gg, terrariamods.wiki.gg) cross-checked against this project's own Phase 4/5 decompiled-source findings (`04-RESEARCH.md`, `05-RESEARCH.md`).

## Scope note

This document only covers bosses that CAN use this project's core mechanism (using a registered summon item redirects the player into a subworld arena). Bosses that structurally cannot — no portable summon item at all; trigger-on-area-entry only; natural rare spawn; a summon item gated behind an NPC questline/field-crafting prerequisite; or — per explicit user decision (2026-08-13) — Wrath of the Gods's bosses regardless of any reusable-item mechanic found for them — are excluded from the goal entirely, not deferred. Section 2 lists them once for traceability and nothing else references them.

Per user direction (2026-08-13 conversation): biome assignment follows what each mod's wiki states as the required/associated biome, even for bosses whose AI does not itself check that biome (e.g. Infernon) — the target is a themed arena matching the wiki's fiction, not strictly the minimum needed to prevent an AI malfunction.

## 1. Mod-combination-sensitive classification — READ FIRST

A boss's assignability is not a fixed property of the boss alone — it depends on which other mods are loaded alongside its owning content mod. The one confirmed case so far:

**Infernum** (internal mod name `InfernumMode`, `weakReferences`/`modReferences`-depends on `CalamityMod`, per its own `build.txt`) rewrites four Calamity bosses' summon mechanics — three get MORE restrictive, one gets LESS restrictive. This cuts both ways and both directions must be handled:

- Providence, Profaned Guardians, Ceaseless Void: "any tile of a given biome" → "a fixed world-generation structure (`The Profaned Garden` in the Underworld, or `Forsaken Archive` under the Dungeon) that cannot be reproduced by placing a portable altar in an empty subworld." Flips ASSIGNABLE → UNASSIGNABLE when Infernum joins.
- The Old Duke: vanilla Calamity has **no summon item at all** (fishing RNG or the Acid Rain event only). Infernum adds a genuinely new, reusable, craftable summon item — **Bloodworm Platter** — used while standing in the Sulphurous Sea. Flips UNASSIGNABLE → ASSIGNABLE when Infernum joins. (Confirmed via `infernummod.wiki.gg/wiki/Bloodworm_Platter`: post-Moon Lord craftable, not consumed on use, directly summons The Old Duke.)

**Implementation requirement to carry into Phase 9 planning:** registration must be gated on `InfernumMode` presence in multiple directions, not just one:
- Providence / Profaned Guardians / Ceaseless Void → register only when `ModLoader.HasMod("CalamityMod") && !ModLoader.HasMod("InfernumMode")`. When Infernum is present, do **not** register these three at all — their summon items must fall through untouched to Infernum's own vanilla-plus-rework `CanUseItem`/`UseItem`, i.e. this mod must not intercept them, not even to show a "can't do this" message. Registering them anyway would let the player teleport into a subworld where the boss cannot actually be summoned (no Profaned Garden/Forsaken Archive structure exists there), producing a silent soft-lock.
- The Old Duke → register (Bloodworm Platter → Sulphurous Sea altar) only when `ModLoader.HasMod("CalamityMod") && ModLoader.HasMod("InfernumMode")`. Without Infernum, there is no summon item to hook at all — nothing to register, not a gate that suppresses an existing registration.
- Astrum Deus / Astrum Aureus → register the Astral altar unconditionally (works with or without Infernum), but when `ModLoader.HasMod("InfernumMode")` is also true, additionally force night in that subworld before/during the summon (same forced-day/night utility need as Section 4's time-gated bosses, just conditional on Infernum here instead of unconditional).

**Open question for discuss-phase:** are there other Calamity-adjacent rework/expansion mods (Fargo's Mod, Fargo's Soul Mod, Calamity Community Remix, etc.) with similar structure-gating effects on any of the bosses currently listed as "remains assignable"? Not audited in this research pass — Infernum is the only rework mod actually checked, because the user raised it specifically. Recommend either a dedicated research pass before Phase 9 scope locks, or explicitly accepting this as a known gap for a later milestone.

## 2. Excluded — Out of Goal

Listed once for traceability; not referenced anywhere else in this document, no altar/arena design effort spent on any of these. (The Old Duke is NOT in this list — see Section 1, it's a mod-combination case.)

- **Mod of Redemption:** Patient Zero, The Janitor, Irradiated Behemoth, Blisterface, MACE Project — each drops/sells a reusable `Hologram Remote`, but it only functions inside that specific boss's fixed arena location (confirmed via the item's own page), so these remain structure-gated, not portable. Protector Volt — no item at all; re-fighting him is an NPC dialogue "Challenge!" option, and he stays fixed in his Sigma Sector room rather than becoming a portable encounter. Skull Digger — natural rare spawn in Caverns post-Keeper, confirmed no resummon item in its full drop list. Calavia — triggers on entering a fixed Underground Portal structure, confirmed no resummon item in its full drop list. Ancient Deity Duo (Ukko & Akka) — the Ancient Sigil summon item exists but only after a field-crafting/charging ritual performed at the Eaglecrest Meadow structure every single time (no reusable-post-unlock path found); confirmed no resummon item in its Treasure Bag drop list either. Re-verified 2026-08-13 by reading each boss's own dedicated wiki page and full drop list directly — CONFIRMED, not just "nothing found."
- **Wrath of the Gods (NoxusBoss):** all 3 bosses excluded per explicit user decision (2026-08-13) — **not assignable, closed, no further investigation.** XG-07 Mars — one-time Draedon-resummon sequence, then reselectable via the Codebreaker's UI. Nameless Deity of Light — one-time Avatar of Emptiness kill, then the reusable `Terminus` item grants repeat access to the Eternal Garden (itself a separate subworld). Avatar of Emptiness — Genesis item, gated behind Solyn's full NPC questline, reusability post-unlock unconfirmed. An earlier research pass had flagged XG-07 Mars and Nameless Deity of Light as "tentatively assignable, pending a code-level save-scope check," since both do have a genuinely portable, reusable unlock item once the one-time story prerequisite is done. The user overruled this after review: all three stay excluded regardless, full stop — this whole mod is out of scope for the altar/arena mechanism, not worth the extra registration-model work (Section 1's `SummonItemRegistry` is keyed on consumable `ItemID` use; XG-07 Mars's UI-option trigger doesn't fit that shape without extra work anyway). Kept as one paragraph here for the research trail, not as an open question — do not re-litigate without new instruction from the user.

Naming follows this project's existing `BossArenaCorruptionSubworld` / `CorruptionPlatformPass` convention (see `Subworlds/BossArenaCorruptionSubworld.cs`, `Subworlds/CorruptionPlatformPass.cs`).

| Biome | Altar item/tile | Arena subworld | Recolor direction | Assigned bosses (final, after Section 1) |

**Astrageldon caveat (CatalystMod, Astral Infection):** independent of biome, Astrageldon's summon item (Astral Communicator) permanently breaks/becomes unusable if the player defeats Moon Lord before killing Astrageldon — a time-window lockout unrelated to biome, not solved by any altar design. Flag for discuss-phase as a separate correctness concern before registering this boss.

**Plain arena, no altar needed at all** (for completeness — not part of this altar document's design work, listed only so Phase 9 doesn't accidentally try to build altars for them): Moon Lord (vanilla), Devourer of Gods, Exo Mechs, Yharon, Supreme Witch Calamitas (Calamity's own "Altar of the Accursed" furniture item just needs to be placeable in the default `BossArenaSubworld`, no new biome altar required), Atlas (Spirit), Omega Obliterator (Redemption, generates its own box arena), Thorn and Seed of Infection (Redemption, low-confidence — wiki states no constraint but entries are sparse).

## 4. Time-gated only — explicitly out of THIS document's mechanism

These bosses need a forced day/night state, not a biome. Different tool (a "force night"/"force day" utility item), not an altar. Kept here only so the day/night requirement isn't lost when Phase 9 scope is defined — do not attempt to solve via biome altar.

- Night: Moon Jelly Wizard, Dusking (Spirit); The Keeper, Omega Cleaver, Omega Gigapora, Nebuleus (Mod of Redemption)
- Day: Fowl Emperor (Mod of Redemption)
- Day OR a Redemption-specific progression stat ("Slayer Star Rating 6", not reproducible via a simple item): King Slayer III

## 5. Open items — needs resolution before Phase 9 planning locks

1. **ContinentOfJourney** — could not be identified across two independent research passes (Steam Workshop search, wiki.gg/fandom search, mirror.sgkoi.dev search). No page, no repo, no Workshop listing found under this exact name. Needs the user to supply an exact Workshop ID or author name, or confirm/correct the mod's actual name, before any further research is possible.
2. **Daybreak** — confirmed to resolve to `gold-meridian/daybreak-mod` on GitHub, described in its own Workshop listing as "a library mod that does nothing on its own... should only be used if a mod depends on it." No boss content exists under this name. If the user intended a different, boss-bearing mod also called "Daybreak," it was not found; needs a corrected name/link.
3. **Bloodworm Platter's Sulphurous Sea requirement — item gate or AI gate?** Not yet determined which layer enforces the biome: if it's only the item's own `CanUseItem` (the way Infernon's `CursedCloth` depth check turned out to be, per `05-RESEARCH.md` Pitfall D), this project's SUBW-04 pipeline bypasses it entirely (never calls the summon item's `CanUseItem`/`UseItem`) and the Sulphurous Sea altar might be unnecessary — a plain arena could work. But The Old Duke's base-Calamity wiki entry already notes it "enrages if it leaves the Sulphurous Sea," which reads as an AI-level dependency, not just an item gate — closer to Hive Mind's `ZoneCorrupt` case than Infernon's. Needs a decompiled-source check before Phase 9 planning decides whether the Sulphurous Sea altar is required or just safe-but-unnecessary for this boss.
4. **Other Calamity-adjacent rework mods** (Fargo's Mod, Fargo's Soul Mod, Calamity Community Remix, etc.) — not audited for structure-gating effects analogous to Infernum's. Only Infernum was researched because the user raised it specifically.
